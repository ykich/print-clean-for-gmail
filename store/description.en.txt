Print only the message body when you use "Print all" in Gmail.

This extension hides the following items on Gmail's print page:
• Account bar (the Gmail logo and your email address)
• Subject
• Sender
• Date
• Recipients and reply-to

HOW TO USE
1. Open an email in Gmail and click the printer icon (Print all).
2. The print preview shows only the message body.
3. To keep some items in the printout, click the extension icon and check the items you want to keep. Your choice is saved right away and also applies to a print page that is already open.

FEATURES
• All five items above are hidden by default.
• Works with single emails and with threads that contain several messages.
• Applies your settings before the print dialog opens (waits up to 1.5 seconds).
• Available in English and Japanese.

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
• Does not read or send your email content. Makes no network requests at all.
• Stores only one setting: which items to keep in the printout. If Chrome sync is on, Chrome syncs this setting across your devices.
• Runs only on Gmail's print page, not on your inbox or other Gmail pages.
• At install time, Chrome shows "Read and change your data on mail.google.com". This is required to hide items on the print page; the extension does not read your email.
• Open source: https://github.com/ykich/print-clean-for-gmail

NOTE
• If loading your settings takes longer than 1.5 seconds, the print dialog opens with the default (all five items hidden).
• If Gmail changes how its print page works, some items may no longer be hidden. Please report problems at https://github.com/ykich/print-clean-for-gmail/issues
